Refunds

If the game is not for you, you get your money back.

Bought on the web

  • The full game costs $7.99, once. Stripe sells it for us through its service Link, and Link sends your receipt.
  • Write to hello@evictnic.com within 14 days of the purchase. Send it from the email on your account, or add the receipt.
  • We refund the full price, no questions asked. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
  • After the refund, the full game closes on your account. Cases 1 and 2 stay free, and your progress in them stays.

Bought in the App Store or on Google Play

  • The store handles refunds under its own rules. We cannot refund a store purchase ourselves.
  • Google Play: open your order history at play.google.com and pick "Request a refund". App Store: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Something broke?

If the unlock did not arrive, or a case does not open, write to hello@evictnic.com. We fix it or refund you, whichever you want.
