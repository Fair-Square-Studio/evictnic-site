Delete your account

You can delete your account and everything it holds, any time, from inside the game.

In the game

  1. Open the Office (the bottom rail, Office).
  2. Tap Delete my account at the bottom of the room.
  3. Tap Tap again to delete to confirm.

This deletes your progress, your account and the full game unlock, from your device and from our server. It cannot be undone. It works the same for a player who never added an email.

By email

If you cannot open the game, write to hello@evictnic.com from the email you used in the game, with the subject Delete my account. We delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What stays

Nothing of the account. A record of a purchase stays with the payment provider (Stripe, Apple or Google) for as long as the law asks them to keep it.
