Privacy

Evict Nic is a story game. It keeps as little about you as it can, and nothing it keeps is for sale. This page says what that is.

What we keep

  • A player id. The game makes one the first time it starts, with no name and no email. That is your account, even before you add an email.
  • Your progress: which cases you finished and which field-report answer you picked in each. No smoking amounts, no health data, no symptoms.
  • Your settings: the portrait, the track (cigarettes, vape or both), the lights.
  • Your email, only if you add one to keep your progress or to sign in on another device.
  • A record of the full-game purchase: what you bought, where (web, App Store or Google Play) and the payment reference. Never a card number: the payment provider keeps that.
  • Technical bits: a one-time pass when you move your progress into an account, the ids of changes the game already saved so a change sent twice counts once, and the steps you took in the game's funnel (a case finished, the offer seen) with no address and no device id.

What we do not keep

  • Nothing from your phone beyond the game: no contacts, no location, no photos, no health app data.
  • No advertising id, no ad network, no analytics company, no tracking across apps or sites.

Who gets it

  • Our own server keeps the game data and runs the sign-in. Resend sends the mail with your sign-in code, so it sees your email address.
  • On the web, Stripe sells the full game for us through its service Link. Stripe keeps your payment details and your billing country (it pays the sales tax for that country). In the stores, Apple or Google keep them.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share your data.

How long

As long as your account exists. Delete the account and everything above goes with it, from our server and from your device. The payment provider keeps its own record of a purchase for as long as the law asks it to.

Delete it

In the game: Office, then Delete my account, twice. Or write to hello@evictnic.com with the subject Delete my account. The delete page has the steps.

Children

Evict Nic is for adults who smoke or vape. It is not for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

When this page changes, the date below changes. Questions: hello@evictnic.com. Evict Nic is a Fair & Square Studio game.
